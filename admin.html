Certificate Management

Check eligibility, generate or upload certificates, and track issued certificates.
🏅 A student is eligible when they have attended at least the class's minimum sessions (default 26 of 32, ≈80%). Late counts as present; sick/excused/absent do not. Generate creates the e-certificate automatically (using your design from the Certificate Template tab, if uploaded); Upload lets you attach a certificate file made elsewhere. Students see issued certificates in their Student Hub.
